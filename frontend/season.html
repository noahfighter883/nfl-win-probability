Predicts point margin with a single regressor; win probability is derived from that same prediction via isotonic calibration, so the two numbers never contradict each other. Backtested on the 2025 season: the model's win-probability Brier score and margin RMSE were statistically indistinguishable from simply using the closing betting line - it is not shown here as having an edge over the market, only as a transparent, from-scratch modeling exercise. Betting lines for games later in the week or season may not be posted yet.
