NFL win probability

One model reads a live game state and says how likely each team is to win right now. A completely different model reads a matchup before kickoff and says who's likely to win before a snap is played. Both are built from scratch, and both pages below show their real, honestly-reported performance — including where they don't beat the obvious baseline.

How the models work

In-game model

Live game state → win probability

  • XGBoost classifier, isotonic-calibrated
  • Trained on ~166k plays from the 2021–2024 seasons
  • Inputs: score differential, time remaining, field position, down & distance, timeouts, and situational flags (red zone, two-minute drill, garbage time, home field)
  • 0.845 AUC on a held-out 2024 season
Powers Historical Replays and the Live Tracker — same trained model, two different data sources (fixed historical plays vs. a live ESPN feed).
Pregame model

Matchup → win probability & spread

  • Ridge regression, isotonic-calibrated
  • Trained on games 2005–2022, calibrated on 2023–2024, tested on 2025
  • Inputs: the betting line, rest days, division game, and situational context. A from-scratch Elo rating system was tried too, but backtested no better than the market line alone — so it isn't part of the shipped model
  • Statistically indistinguishable from the closing line on the 2025 holdout, reported as-is rather than oversold
Powers Season Predictions — a genuinely different model, not a variant of the in-game one.
